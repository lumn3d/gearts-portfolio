# LUMN · GEARTS Portfolio

Art Appreciation portfolio for Jann Railey De Chavez. Static HTML/CSS, published from main through GitHub Pages.

## Portfolio sequence
1. **Part I — My Art Appreciation Journey:** all individual and group GEARTS outputs, curated in a meaningful order.
2. **Part II — My Artistic Exploration:** 3–5 artworks exploring forms beyond usual academic or everyday practice. Three clearly labelled placeholders are prepared; add up to two more.
3. **Final Reflection — Looking Forward: Who Am I Becoming?** Intentionally blank.
4. **Appendices:** proof of creation for Part II, organized by artwork.
5. **Consent Form:** intentionally blank. This is a separate document; no consent choice has been made.

## Upload folders
- `assets/part-1/individual/`: individual course activities.
- `assets/part-1/group/`: collaborative course activities.
- `assets/part-2/artwork-01/artwork/`, `artwork-02/artwork/`, `artwork-03/artwork/`: finished works.
- The corresponding `process/` folders: sketches, progress images, recordings and other authentic proof of creation.

Create subject folders within Part I when needed. Keep all views of one subject together. For a fourth or fifth exploration, copy the same artwork/process folder structure.

Uploading alone does **not** create a card: edit `index.html` to connect each file, or ask for uploaded assets to be connected. Use meaningful alt text for images, native playback controls for recordings, captions/transcripts where needed, and descriptive download links for documents. Preserve natural image proportions. Replace placeholder copy with the artwork title, medium, date and relevant context. For group work, credit collaborators and state your contribution.

## Part II requirements
- 3–5 artworks; only ONE may be previously created.
- For that previously created work, establish authorship and explain its connection to your identity.
- New artworks must not be AI-generated or AI-assisted.
- No literary works (including poems and short stories).
- Include authentic creative-process proof for every new work, displayed after the final reflection in the appendices.
- Eligible forms include painting, drawing, sculpture, crafts, music, dance, performance and photography.
- No reflection is required for every artwork.
- The final reflection should discuss your journey, discoveries, struggles and identity development. It remains blank for you to write.
- A separate consent form is required. Permission to use work as evidence of learning or in exhibitions is optional and does not affect the grade. Use the actual course form when supplied.

The Task Guide and Consent Form attachments were not supplied with this update. No replacement consent form or student reflection has been invented.
